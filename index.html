What we know so far about j3llyf1sh? This species swarms in human-frequented waters. It is highly friendly to humans and will follow your finger(mouse) to swim, but unknown marine pollutants have infected it, carrying potential mutation risks. But don't worry, they can heal themselves easily, it just takes a little time.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature You can put your finger in the water, and they will follow your finger. If you accidentally pop the bubbles containing mysterious substances, they will immediately undergo unpredictable mutations.
